Visa Guide for Pakistani Passport Holders

Clear, up-to-date visa requirements for 10 popular destinations. Plan your travel with confidence.

Disclaimer: Visa rules change frequently. Always verify with the official embassy or consulate of the destination country before applying. This page is for informational purposes only.
🇦🇪

United Arab Emirates

e-Visa / Visa on Arrival*
  • Visa Required: Yes (most cases)
  • Type: Tourist e-Visa or Visa on Arrival (selected nationalities / packages)
  • Validity: Usually 30 or 60 days
  • Processing: 3–5 working days (e-Visa)
  • Key Documents: Passport (6+ months validity), photo, hotel booking, return ticket, bank statement
  • Apply via: Official UAE ICP portal or approved travel agents / airlines

*Visa on Arrival is available only under specific conditions. Most Pakistani citizens need a pre-arranged visa.

🇸🇦

Saudi Arabia

e-Visa / Visit Visa
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: Tourist e-Visa or Visit Visa
  • Validity: Up to 1 year (multiple entry possible)
  • Processing: Usually 24–72 hours for e-Visa
  • Key Documents: Passport, photo, proof of accommodation, return ticket, financial means
  • Apply via: Official Saudi eVisa portal (visitsaudi.com)

Umrah and Hajj visas follow separate processes and seasons.

🇹🇷

Turkey

e-Visa
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: e-Visa (Tourist)
  • Validity: Usually 30–90 days single/multiple entry
  • Processing: Often within minutes to 24 hours
  • Key Documents: Passport, email, payment card, travel details
  • Apply via: Official Turkish e-Visa website (evisa.gov.tr)

Ensure you use only the official government site to avoid scams.

🇲🇾

Malaysia

eNTRI / e-Visa
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: eNTRI (for tourism) or e-Visa
  • Validity: Typically 15–30 days
  • Processing: 1–3 working days
  • Key Documents: Passport, photo, return ticket, hotel booking, bank statement
  • Apply via: Official Malaysian Immigration / eVisa portals

Rules can change; always check the latest eNTRI eligibility.

🇹🇭

Thailand

Embassy / Consulate Visa
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: Tourist Visa (TR) or Visa on Arrival (limited cases)
  • Validity: Usually 60 days (single entry TR)
  • Processing: 3–7 working days at embassy/consulate
  • Key Documents: Passport, photos, application form, bank statement, return ticket, hotel booking, invitation (if any)
  • Apply via: Royal Thai Embassy / Consulate in Pakistan

Visa Exemption or VOA is not generally available for Pakistani passports.

🇶🇦

Qatar

Visa-Free / Hayya (events) / VOA options
  • Visa Required: Often not required for short stays under certain conditions
  • Type: Visa-free entry or Hayya platform (for events / tourism packages)
  • Validity: Usually up to 30 days
  • Processing: Pre-approval via Hayya or on-arrival in eligible cases
  • Key Documents: Passport, return ticket, proof of accommodation, sufficient funds
  • Apply via: Hayya platform or check Qatar Airways / MOI guidelines

Always confirm current entry rules before travel — policies have been updated multiple times.

🇬🇧

United Kingdom

Standard Visitor Visa
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: Standard Visitor Visa
  • Validity: Up to 6 months (longer visas possible)
  • Processing: Usually 3–6 weeks (can vary)
  • Key Documents: Passport, application, biometrics, bank statements, employment letter, ties to Pakistan, travel itinerary
  • Apply via: Official UK Visas and Immigration (gov.uk) + VAC appointment

Strong proof of intention to return is critical for approval.

🇺🇸

United States

B1/B2 Visitor Visa
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: B1/B2 (Business/Tourist)
  • Validity: Usually multiple entry, up to 10 years (stay limited per visit)
  • Processing: Interview wait times vary significantly
  • Key Documents: DS-160, passport, photo, appointment confirmation, strong ties evidence, financial proof
  • Apply via: CEAC / ustraveldocs + Embassy/Consulate interview

Interview is mandatory. Refusal rates can be high without clear ties to home country.

🇨🇦

Canada

Visitor Visa (TRV)
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: Temporary Resident Visa (Visitor)
  • Validity: Up to 10 years multiple entry possible
  • Processing: Several weeks to months
  • Key Documents: Passport, application, biometrics, bank statements, employment, invitation letter (if any), travel history
  • Apply via: IRCC online portal + VFS Global biometrics

Biometrics are required. Processing times fluctuate — check IRCC tool.

🇪🇺

Schengen Area (Europe)

Schengen Visa (Type C)
  • Visa Required: Yes
  • Type: Short-stay Schengen Visa (Type C)
  • Validity: Up to 90 days in any 180-day period
  • Processing: Usually 15–30 days (can be longer)
  • Key Documents: Passport, form, photos, travel insurance (€30k+), itinerary, hotel bookings, bank statements, employment letter
  • Apply via: Embassy/Consulate of main destination country or VFS Global

Apply to the country of main destination or first entry. Travel insurance is mandatory.

General Application Tips for Pakistani Applicants

1

Check Official Sources

Always start from the official government or embassy website. Avoid third-party sites that charge extra for the same service.

2

Prepare Strong Documentation

Bank statements (3–6 months), employment letter, property documents, and previous travel history significantly improve chances.

3

Show Ties to Pakistan

Embassies look for clear reasons you will return — job, family, business, or studies in Pakistan.

4

Apply Early

Especially for UK, USA, Canada and Schengen. Appointment slots and processing can take weeks or months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Pakistani citizens need a visa for every country?

No. A few destinations offer visa-free entry or visa on arrival under specific conditions (e.g. certain Gulf policies). However, most popular destinations require a visa or e-Visa.

Is an e-Visa the same as a regular visa?

An e-Visa is an electronic visa issued online. It is still an official visa — you do not need to visit an embassy in most cases, but you must still meet all eligibility requirements.

How long should my passport be valid?

Most countries require at least 6 months validity from the date of entry (or from the date of application). Always check the specific rule for your destination.

Can I work on a tourist visa?

No. Tourist and visitor visas do not allow employment. Working without the correct visa can lead to bans and deportation.
